Privacy Policy

Varia is an independent iOS app that helps you discover YouTube channels. It is built and operated by a single developer. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: Varia collects the minimum needed to keep your account and your list working, plus anonymous-by-default usage statistics so I can tell which parts of the app people actually use. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers or data brokers. The app carries no advertising at all; the varia.life website shows Google AdSense ads — see section 12.

1. Using Varia without an account

You can browse Varia without signing in. In guest mode, the channels you follow (up to five) and your preferences are stored only on your device. They are not sent to any server and I cannot see them.

Usage statistics and crash reports described in section 4 are still collected in guest mode, but they are not connected to any identity.

3. YouTube subscriptions import

If you sign in with Google, Varia asks for read-only permission to your YouTube subscriptions. If you grant it, the app reads the channel identifiers you are subscribed to — nothing else. No watch history, no playlists, no comments, no videos.

These identifiers are used as an interest signal to improve which channels are recommended to you and to users with similar taste. They are never added to your follow list and are never shown to other users.

You can decline this permission and still use every other part of Varia. You can withdraw it at any time from your Google account permissions page, and you can remove the imported data entirely by deleting your Varia account (section 8).

4. Usage statistics and crash reports

Varia uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records which screens are opened and which actions are taken — a channel opened, a channel followed, the dice rolled, a search performed, onboarding completed. Crashlytics records technical details when the app crashes: device model, iOS version, and the error itself.

A few of those interaction events — opening a channel, following it, or opening it on YouTube — are also stored on Varia's own servers, tied to your account. This is what makes your recommendations personal: what you actually do in the app turns out to describe your taste far better than the topics you pick during setup. These records are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically, and they go immediately if you delete your account. Nothing else about your activity is stored this way — no search text, no browsing history.

Two things worth knowing:

  • Search text is never sent. When you search, the app records only how many characters you typed and how many results came back — never what you typed.
  • There is no advertising identifier. The app does not read the IDFA, does not include any advertising framework, and will never show you an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track.

When you are signed in, your account identifier is attached to these reports so a crash can be traced to the account that hit it. In guest mode no identifier is attached.

6. How your data is protected

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data, including any data obtained through Google APIs.

  • Encrypted in transit. Every connection between the app, the Varia API and the database uses HTTPS/TLS. The app makes no plain HTTP requests.
  • Encrypted at rest. The database is hosted on Supabase, where stored data and backups are encrypted at rest.
  • Your Google token is never stored. When you import your subscriptions, the access token is held in memory only for the duration of that single request, used to call the YouTube Data API, and then discarded. It is never written to the database, to a log or to a file.
  • Row-level access control. Every table holding user data has row-level security enabled in the database, so one account cannot read another account's rows.
  • Authenticated requests only. Endpoints that touch your data require a signed session token, which the server verifies on every request.
  • Rate limiting. Requests are rate limited to reduce the risk of abuse and automated scraping.
  • Secrets stay on the server. API keys and database credentials are kept in the server environment — never in the app binary, never in the public source repository.
  • Least privilege. Varia asks YouTube for read-only access and keeps only the channel identifiers it needs. It never requests write access, so it cannot change anything in your YouTube account.

No system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure. If you believe you have found a security problem in Varia, please write to ibrahimdn250@gmail.com.

7. Varia and YouTube

Varia uses YouTube API Services to gather public information about channels — names, descriptions, subscriber counts, video counts, thumbnails and recent video titles.

By using Varia you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can review and revoke Varia's access to your Google data at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Varia is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: Profile → Delete account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your follow list, your preferences and your imported YouTube subscription identifiers. This cannot be undone. Usage statistics already collected are retained by Firebase according to Google's own retention schedule and are no longer connected to you once the account is gone.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase the data held about you, and to object to how it is processed. Deleting your account inside the app satisfies erasure immediately. For anything else, write to the address below and I will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

Varia is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided information, write to me and I will delete it.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top will change and the updated version will be published here before the change takes effect.

12. Advertising on the varia.life website

The iOS app shows no ads. It contains no advertising framework, does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), and collects nothing for advertising purposes.

The varia.life website shows ads through Google AdSense. To serve them, Google and its partners may set cookies and process information sent by your browser, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. That processing is governed by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can turn off personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings.

Ads appear in one fixed position on the page; Google is not allowed to place them wherever it likes. Your YouTube data is never used to target ads — neither the subscriptions you import nor the channels you follow. That is also a requirement of the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

Varia, Google Firebase Analytics ve Firebase Crashlytics kullanıyor. Analytics hangi ekranların açıldığını ve hangi eylemlerin yapıldığını kaydediyor: kanal açma, kanal takip etme, zar atma, arama yapma, kuruluma başlama ve bitirme gibi. Bu etkileşimlerden birkaçı — bir kanalı açman, takip etmen veya YouTube'da açman — Varia'nın kendi sunucusunda da hesabına bağlı olarak saklanıyor. Önerilerini kişisel kılan şey bu: uygulamada gerçekten ne yaptığın, kurulumda seçtiğin konulardan çok daha iyi anlatıyor neyden hoşlandığını. 6. Verin nasıl korunuyor

Verinin gizliliğini korumak için güvenlik önlemleri alınmış durumda; Google API'lerinden gelen veri de buna dahil.

  • İletimde şifreli. Uygulama, Varia API'si ve veritabanı arasındaki her bağlantı HTTPS/TLS kullanıyor. Uygulama düz HTTP isteği atmıyor.
  • Depoda şifreli. Veritabanı Supabase üzerinde; orada saklanan veri ve yedekler şifreli tutuluyor.
  • Google tokenın hiç saklanmıyor. Aboneliklerini içe aktarırken erişim tokenı yalnızca o tek isteğin süresince bellekte tutuluyor, YouTube Data API'sini çağırmak için kullanılıyor ve sonra atılıyor. Veritabanına, log'a veya bir dosyaya asla yazılmıyor.
  • Satır bazında erişim denetimi. Kullanıcı verisi tutan her tabloda satır düzeyinde güvenlik açık; bir hesap başka bir hesabın satırlarını okuyamıyor.
  • Yalnızca kimliği doğrulanmış istekler. Verine dokunan uçlar imzalı bir oturum tokenı istiyor ve sunucu bunu her istekte doğruluyor.
  • Hız sınırı. İstekler hız sınırına tabi; kötüye kullanım ve otomatik kazıma riskini azaltıyor.
  • Sırlar sunucuda kalıyor. API anahtarları ve veritabanı kimlik bilgileri sunucunun ortam değişkenlerinde duruyor — uygulama ikilisinde de, açık kaynak deposunda da yok.
  • En az yetki. Varia YouTube'dan yalnızca okuma yetkisi istiyor ve yalnızca ihtiyaç duyduğu kanal kimliklerini tutuyor. Yazma yetkisi hiç istenmiyor, yani YouTube hesabında hiçbir şeyi değiştiremez.

Hiçbir sistemin kusursuz güvenli olduğu garanti edilemez. Varia'da bir güvenlik sorunu bulduğunu düşünüyorsan ibrahimdn250@gmail.com adresine yaz.
