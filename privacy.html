Privacy Policy

Varia is an independent iOS app that helps you discover YouTube channels. It is built and operated by a single developer. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: Varia collects the minimum needed to keep your account and your list working, plus anonymous-by-default usage statistics so I can tell which parts of the app people actually use. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

1. Using Varia without an account

You can browse Varia without signing in. In guest mode, the channels you follow (up to five) and your preferences are stored only on your device. They are not sent to any server and I cannot see them.

Usage statistics and crash reports described in section 4 are still collected in guest mode, but they are not connected to any identity.

3. YouTube subscriptions import

If you sign in with Google, Varia asks for read-only permission to your YouTube subscriptions. If you grant it, the app reads the channel identifiers you are subscribed to — nothing else. No watch history, no playlists, no comments, no videos.

These identifiers are used as an interest signal to improve which channels are recommended to you and to users with similar taste. They are never added to your follow list and are never shown to other users.

You can decline this permission and still use every other part of Varia. You can withdraw it at any time from your Google account permissions page, and you can remove the imported data entirely by deleting your Varia account (section 7).

4. Usage statistics and crash reports

Varia uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records which screens are opened and which actions are taken — a channel opened, a channel followed, the dice rolled, a search performed, onboarding completed. Crashlytics records technical details when the app crashes: device model, iOS version, and the error itself.

A few of those interaction events — opening a channel, following it, or opening it on YouTube — are also stored on Varia's own servers, tied to your account. This is what makes your recommendations personal: what you actually do in the app turns out to describe your taste far better than the topics you pick during setup. These records are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically, and they go immediately if you delete your account. Nothing else about your activity is stored this way — no search text, no browsing history.

Two things worth knowing:

  • Search text is never sent. When you search, the app records only how many characters you typed and how many results came back — never what you typed.
  • There is no advertising identifier. Varia does not read the IDFA, does not include any advertising framework, and will never show you an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track.

When you are signed in, your account identifier is attached to these reports so a crash can be traced to the account that hit it. In guest mode no identifier is attached.

6. Varia and YouTube

Varia uses YouTube API Services to gather public information about channels — names, descriptions, subscriber counts, video counts, thumbnails and recent video titles.

By using Varia you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can review and revoke Varia's access to your Google data at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Varia is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: Profile → Delete account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your follow list, your preferences and your imported YouTube subscription identifiers. This cannot be undone. Usage statistics already collected are retained by Firebase according to Google's own retention schedule and are no longer connected to you once the account is gone.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase the data held about you, and to object to how it is processed. Deleting your account inside the app satisfies erasure immediately. For anything else, write to the address below and I will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

Varia is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided information, write to me and I will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top will change and the updated version will be published here before the change takes effect.

3. YouTube aboneliklerinin içe aktarılması

Google ile giriş yaparsan Varia, YouTube aboneliklerini yalnızca okumak için izin istiyor. İzin verirsen uygulama, abone olduğun kanalların kimliklerini okuyor — başka hiçbir şeyi değil. İzleme geçmişi, oynatma listesi, yorum, video: hiçbiri okunmuyor.

Bu kimlikler, sana ve benzer zevkteki kullanıcılara hangi kanalların önerileceğini iyileştirmek için bir ilgi sinyali olarak kullanılıyor. Takip listene asla eklenmiyor ve başka kullanıcılara hiçbir zaman gösterilmiyor.

Bu izni vermeyi reddedip Varia'nın geri kalanını sorunsuz kullanabilirsin. İzni dilediğin an Google hesap izinleri sayfasından geri alabilir, içe aktarılan veriyi de Varia hesabını silerek tamamen kaldırabilirsin (7. bölüm).

4. Kullanım istatistikleri ve çökme raporları

Varia, Google Firebase Analytics ve Firebase Crashlytics kullanıyor. Analytics hangi ekranların açıldığını ve hangi eylemlerin yapıldığını kaydediyor: kanal açma, kanal takip etme, zar atma, arama yapma, kuruluma başlama ve bitirme gibi. Crashlytics ise uygulama çöktüğünde teknik ayrıntıları kaydediyor: cihaz modeli, iOS sürümü ve hatanın kendisi.

Bu etkileşimlerden birkaçı — bir kanalı açman, takip etmen veya YouTube'da açman — Varia'nın kendi sunucusunda da hesabına bağlı olarak saklanıyor. Önerilerini kişisel kılan şey bu: uygulamada gerçekten ne yaptığın, kurulumda seçtiğin konulardan çok daha iyi anlatıyor neyden hoşlandığını. Bu kayıtlar 90 gün tutuluyor, sonra kendiliğinden siliniyor; hesabını silersen anında gidiyor. Hareketlerinle ilgili başka hiçbir şey böyle saklanmıyor — arama metni yok, gezinme geçmişi yok.

Bilmen gereken iki şey:

  • Arama metni hiçbir zaman gönderilmiyor. Arama yaptığında uygulama yalnızca kaç karakter yazdığını ve kaç sonuç döndüğünü kaydediyor — ne yazdığını değil.
  • Reklam kimliği yok. Varia IDFA'yı okumuyor, herhangi bir reklam kütüphanesi içermiyor ve sana asla App Tracking Transparency izin penceresi göstermeyecek — çünkü takip edilecek bir şey yok.

Giriş yaptığında hesap kimliğin bu raporlara ekleniyor; böylece bir çökme, onu yaşayan hesaba kadar izlenebiliyor. Misafir modunda hiçbir kimlik eklenmiyor.
